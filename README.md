## 📖 Project Overview

The **Vrinda Store Data Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze sales data and transform raw information into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard helps analyze **sales trends, customer demographics, product categories, sales channels, and geographical performance** through interactive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and order performance.
* Identify monthly sales trends.
* Analyze customer demographics.
* Identify top-performing sales channels.
* Analyze product category performance.
* Identify state-wise sales performance.
* Support data-driven business decisions.
